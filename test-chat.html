Session Variables
Slack channel and thread

Shared with the Patch Testing Scrub screen, so setting it in either place sets it in both. The token itself is saved on the site and is managed on the scrub screen.

Attendance

Reads who has posted since you pressed Start, so the roll call matches the meeting rather than the last twelve hours. Start the timer first, then pull.

Announcements from WordPress

The latest posts from the Make WordPress blogs, so section 7 does not need a separate tab open. Pick one to fill the box, or type your own.

Meeting notes

Builds the skeleton of the notes post from what this screen already knows: the agenda link, who ran it, who was there, and whatever you jot down as it happens. Nothing here is sent anywhere until you press a button.
