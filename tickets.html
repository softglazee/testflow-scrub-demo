Tickets

Every ticket currently in the pool, grouped by where it came from. Detail columns are filled in for tickets imported from a Trac CSV export or synced from GitHub. Tickets typed in by hand stay blank, because Trac refuses machine requests and the plugin cannot look one up on its own.

Tested by means people this console assigned the ticket to, across every session in this browser. It is not a reading of the Trac ticket, so somebody who tested it independently will not appear.
